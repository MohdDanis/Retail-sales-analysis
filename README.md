# Retail Sales & Profit Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

An end-to-end retail sales and profit analysis project developed using **Microsoft Excel and Power BI**.

The project starts with a raw Excel dataset, progresses through data cleaning and business analysis, and culminates in an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to communicate sales and profitability performance through meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to understand:

- Where sales and profit are being generated
- Which product categories contribute most to performance
- Which customer segments generate the highest sales
- How sales vary across regions and years
- How shipping modes and order priorities relate to sales
- Which areas of the business show stronger or weaker performance

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains:

- **8,399 transaction-level records**
- **21 fields**
- Order and customer information
- Product and category information
- Geographic information
- Sales and profit metrics
- Discount and shipping information
- Order priority and shipping mode

The cleaned dataset was validated for data quality, with no blank values detected in the inspected fields and no exact duplicate rows identified. Date fields were also normalized for consistent analysis.

## 🔍 Business Analysis

### Regional Performance

The **West** region generated the highest sales at approximately **3.60M**, followed by Ontario at approximately 3.06M and Prarie at approximately 2.84M.

### Product Category Performance

**Technology** was the strongest category, generating approximately **5.98M in sales** and **886K in profit**.

### Customer Segment

The **Corporate** segment generated the highest sales at approximately **5.50M**, followed by Home Office and Consumer.

### Shipping Mode

**Regular Air** accounted for approximately **7.51M in sales**, followed by Delivery Truck and Express Air.

### Order Priority

Low-priority orders recorded approximately **3.28M in sales**, narrowly ahead of High-priority orders at approximately 3.27M.

### Yearly Performance

**2009** recorded the highest annual sales at approximately **4.21M**, followed by 2012 at approximately 3.72M.

### Top Products

The Top-10 product analysis identified products collectively contributing approximately **1.78M in sales**.
